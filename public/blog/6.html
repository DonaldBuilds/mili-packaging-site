What Is a Magnetic Closure Box?

A magnetic closure box is a rigid setup box with magnets embedded in the flap and the box body. When closed, the magnets align and hold the flap securely. When opened, the flap lifts smoothly without the tearing or wear of traditional tuck-flap boxes.

Key components:

  • Greyboard core: 1.5-3mm, provides structural rigidity
  • Wrapping paper: 128-200gsm art paper, kraft, or specialty textured paper
  • Magnets: 2-4 neodymium disc magnets (8-12mm diameter), embedded between greyboard layers
  • Hinge: Paper or fabric hinge connecting lid to base

Why Luxury Brands Choose Magnetic Boxes

1. Perceived Value

The weight and solidity of a magnetic box signals quality before the product is seen. Studies show that heavier packaging increases perceived product value by 25-35%. The magnetic closure itself — that satisfying click — creates a multi-sensory brand experience.

2. Reusability

Unlike tuck-flap boxes that tear and wear with repeated opening, magnetic boxes are designed to be opened and closed hundreds of times. Customers keep them as storage, extending brand exposure for months or years after purchase.

3. Protection

The rigid structure protects fragile products during shipping. Combined with a custom foam or velvet insert, a magnetic box provides superior protection compared to folding cartons.

Design Options for Magnetic Boxes

Flap Styles

  • Full flap: Lid covers entire top surface. Most common, most protective.
  • Half flap: Lid covers half the top. Creates a "reveal" effect. Popular for jewelry.
  • Book-style: Opens like a book cover. Dramatic reveal for premium unboxing.

Interior Options

  • Custom foam insert (EVA): Precision-cut to hold each product. Best for fragile items.
  • Velvet lining: Adds luxury tactile sensation. Popular for jewelry and watches. See our Watch Display Boxes.
  • Cardboard divider: Cost-effective for multi-product sets. Our Luxury Gift Boxes include custom inserts.

FAQ

Q: How strong are the magnets in magnetic closure boxes?

A: We use 8-12mm neodymium magnets that provide 200-400 grams of holding force each. Two magnets typically provide enough force to keep the flap securely closed during shipping and handling.

Q: Can magnetic boxes be shipped internationally?

A: Yes. The magnets are small and do not interfere with shipping regulations. Mili Packaging ships magnetic boxes to 50+ countries via DDP, FOB, and CIF. We have never had a customs issue related to magnets.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom magnetic boxes?

A: MOQ starts at 100 pieces. For orders under 500 pieces, we recommend standard sizes to minimize per-unit cost. Custom dimensions are available for orders of 500+ pieces.

Q: How long do the magnets last?

A: Neodymium magnets lose less than 1% of their strength per decade under normal conditions. For packaging applications, the magnets will outlast the box itself and remain functional for the entire lifespan of the product.
