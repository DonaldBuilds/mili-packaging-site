Shipping Packaging 101: How to Protect Products and Impress Customers

Published Aug 4, 2026 · 6 min read · By Mili Packaging

Shipping packaging serves two masters: protection and presentation. The box must survive conveyor belts, stacking, and the occasional drop from 1.2 meters (the standard UPS/FedEx drop test height). But it is also the first physical touchpoint your customer has with your brand. This guide covers material selection, structural design, printing, and cost optimization for B2B buyers sourcing shipping packaging.

1. Choose the Right Corrugated Material

Corrugated board comes in different flute types, each with different strength, weight, and printability characteristics.

FluteThicknessBest For
B-Flute2.5-3.0mmMost versatile; good print surface; die-cut mailers
C-Flute3.5-4.0mmHeavier items; shipping cartons; less print detail
E-Flute1.5-1.8mmLightweight items; best print quality; premium feel
BC Double-Wall6.0-7.0mmHeavy/fragile items; maximum protection

For B2B e-commerce: B-Flute or E-Flute mailer boxes offer the best balance of protection, print quality, and cost. Our Custom Mailer Boxes use B-Flute and E-Flute.

2. Structural Design: The Right Box Style

Regular Slotted Container (RSC)

Standard shipping box with four flaps. Most economical. Best for bulk B2B shipments where the box itself is not customer-facing.

Die-Cut Mailer Box

Custom-shaped box with interlocking flaps. No tape needed. Creates a premium unboxing experience. Our mailer boxes ship flat and assemble in seconds.

Full-Overlap Box

Flaps fully overlap on top and bottom. Maximum stacking strength. Best for heavy products (5kg+).

Telescoping Box

Two-piece box (lid + base). Premium feel, excellent protection. Often used for gift-with-purchase and subscription boxes.

3. Internal Protection

External box damage is cosmetic. Internal product damage is a refund. Invest in internal protection before upgrading external material.

  • Custom insert: Die-cut cardboard or foam that holds each product securely. Reduces movement during transit.
  • Kraft paper void fill: Crumpled kraft for lightweight items. Eco-friendly. Not suitable for heavy items.
  • Bubble wrap: For fragile items. Adds cost but prevents breakage on glass and ceramics.
  • Air pillows: Lightweight void fill. Reduces shipping weight vs paper fill.
  • Molded pulp: Eco-friendly alternative to foam. Made from recycled paper.

4. Printing: Brand Your Shipping Box

An unbranded brown box is a missed marketing opportunity. Every package shipped is a billboard traveling across the country.

  • Flexo printing: 1-3 colors, best for logos and simple designs. Cost-effective. Standard on our Shipping Boxes.
  • Digital printing: Full-color, no plate costs. Ideal for small runs (100-500 boxes).
  • Offset litho-lamination: High-quality print on a separate sheet, then laminated to corrugated. Best print quality but higher cost.
  • Inside printing: A surprise message or pattern printed on the inside of the box. Creates a social media moment when customers discover it.

5. Sustainability in Shipping Packaging

  • FSC-certified corrugated: Responsibly sourced materials. Mili Packaging is FSC certified.
  • Recycled content: 30-100% post-consumer recycled corrugated available.
  • Right-sizing: Eliminate empty space to reduce material use and shipping cost.
  • Soy-based inks: Biodegradable printing inks. Read our sustainable packaging guide.

6. Cost Optimization for Shipping Boxes

  • Flat-packed vs pre-assembled: Flat-packed mailer boxes save 60-80% on storage and inbound freight. All Mili mailer boxes ship flat.
  • Standard vs custom sizes: Standard sizes (from our catalog) eliminate die-cutting costs.
  • Single-wall vs double-wall: Single-wall is 30-40% cheaper. Only use double-wall for items over 5kg or extremely fragile products.
  • Order quantity: MOQ 1000 units but per-unit cost drops 40% at 5,000+ units. More cost reduction tips.

FAQ

Q: What is the difference between corrugated and cardboard?

A: Corrugated has a fluted (wavy) inner layer between two flat sheets, providing strength. Cardboard (chipboard) is a single layer without fluting and is thinner. Shipping boxes use corrugated; gift boxes use cardboard or greyboard.

Q: How do I determine the right corrugated strength for my product?

A: Use this guideline: under 1kg = E-Flute or B-Flute. 1-5kg = B-Flute. 5-10kg = C-Flute or BC double-wall. Over 10kg = BC double-wall with additional internal protection.

Q: Can shipping boxes look premium enough for direct-to-consumer delivery?

A: Yes. E-Flute die-cut mailer boxes with full-color digital printing and a custom insert rival the unboxing experience of rigid gift boxes, at approximately half the cost. Many DTC skincare brands use this approach.
